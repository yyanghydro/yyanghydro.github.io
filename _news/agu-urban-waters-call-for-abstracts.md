We welcome abstracts for AGU Annual Meeting 2026 session **H092: Intelligent and Resilient Urban Water Systems - Bridging Monitoring, Modeling, and Management in Coupled Human-Natural Systems**.

The session invites researchers and practitioners working across the urban water domain. Topics include monitoring and sensing, process modeling and machine learning, stormwater and water quality, water supply and reuse, green infrastructure and nature-based solutions, and hydroclimatic risk and decision-making under uncertainty.

**Abstract deadline:** August 5, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. EDT (August 6 at 03:59 UTC)

**Meeting:** December 7-11, 2026, San Francisco, California

[View session details and submit an abstract](https://agu.confex.com/agu/agu26/prelim.cgi/Session/281767){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}
