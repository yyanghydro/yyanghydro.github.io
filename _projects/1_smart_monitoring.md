This project will develop and test an AI-enabled, smartphone-based monitoring workflow for green stormwater infrastructure. Students, residents, community groups, and stormwater managers will be able to take standardized site photos and field notes that help identify maintenance needs.

The workflow will use vision-language models to screen for visible concerns such as blocked inlets, sediment buildup, unhealthy vegetation, litter, standing water, and erosion. Surveys in Boston and New York City, together with focused hydrologic checks at selected Boston sites, will connect visual conditions with drainage performance. The goal is to detect maintenance needs earlier and help rain gardens, bioswales, and related systems function effectively over time.

This project is conducted in collaboration with and supported by the [Stone Living Lab (SLL)](https://stonelivinglab.org/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.
